# VendiStep Economy

## Roll Tokens

Roll Tokens are the primary game currency.

One Roll Token allows the player to perform one roll on a vending
machine.

## Daily Login Reward

The player receives:

- 1 Roll Token for logging in each day

A player can receive the daily reward once per day.

## Step Rewards

The player earns:

- 1 Roll Token for every 5,000 steps

Examples:

| Steps | Roll Tokens |
|------:|------------:|
| 4,999 | 0 |
| 5,000 | 1 |
| 10,000 | 2 |
| 15,000 | 3 |
| 20,000 | 4 |

Unfinished step progress normally resets at the end of the day.

## Weekly Login Reward

Logging in on at least 4 of 7 days awards:

- 1 Step Freeze

## Step Freeze

A Step Freeze preserves unfinished progress toward the player's
next 5,000-step reward.

Example:

A player walks 12,500 steps.

- 10,000 steps have already awarded 2 Roll Tokens.
- 2,500 steps remain toward the next reward.
- Using a Step Freeze carries those 2,500 steps into the next day.

Already-rewarded steps cannot be carried over or rewarded again.

## Paid Currency

Planned pricing:

- 4 Roll Tokens — $0.99
- 1 Step Freeze — approximately $0.99

Pricing should be tested before final release.

## Rarity

Current planned rarity distribution:

| Rarity | Roll Chance |
|---|---:|
| Legendary | 1% |
| Rare | 19% |
| Uncommon | 30% |
| Common | 50% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

The rarity tier is selected first.

After selecting the tier, every item within that tier has an equal
chance of being selected.

## Duplicate Protection

Receiving a duplicate increases the chance of receiving a
non-duplicate item on the next roll by 5%.
